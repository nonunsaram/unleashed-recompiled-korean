
## v1.0.2 changes

- Corrected the Korean transcription of Egg Dragoon throughout the game.
- Fixed the subtitle atlas builder treating upper- and lower-case Latin letters as the same glyph. `Excellent!` now keeps its lower-case `e` characters.
- Added 2x Korean world-map assets for UnleasHD 1.4.2. In the HMM mod configuration, set **UnleasHD compatibility** to **UnleasHD 1.4.2** and place the Korean patch above UnleasHD.

Crimson Carnival over Eggmanland and Windmill Isle Act 5 ship their own added `StageList_list.fco` tables. Their added act names are outside this compatibility option.

- Added four title-logo choices, including World Adventure with a left-aligned Korean caption and stroke-shaped white glow. Keep the Korean patch above graphics mods.
